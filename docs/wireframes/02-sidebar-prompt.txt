Rules
- Opening: template.apply with followUp: true (use re-checked, no apply event, no first-name lookup).
- Insert: template.apply once without followUp (one apply event); a re-rendered card carries counted=1 and
  sends followUp: true, so one card counts once.
- Values: a typed value wins; a blank field takes the value the Insert call resolves (recipient first name),
  else it is removed with the 4 space rules (ADR-0004 Amendment 2026-11-11). Over 500 characters blocks.
- Subject only while "Replace the subject" is on; recipients only while "Add recipients" is on. Both off =
  the old "Insert body only". Switch on with a filled draft subject = the old "Insert with subject".
- One UpdateDraftActionResponse: body, subject (switch), To, Cc, Bcc (spec 5.7). Never setSendStatus.
  The card closes after the insert (spike S6 step 0); no notification is possible.
- Removed against the slice 9 cards: "Fill these fields to insert", "Fields to fill: ...", "Sets the draft
  subject.", "The draft already has a subject. It is kept.", [Insert with subject], [Insert body only], [Cancel],
  the too-many-fields card, the "Review" row button.

Side panel (entries inbox and message, until spike S7): the same card with no Fields section, no switches and
no [Insert]; the recipient lines read as for an empty draft; the footer hint of spec 7.2 is the last section.
After S7 the footer primary is the compose action ([Create email] or [Create reply]), ADR-0009 section 6.
